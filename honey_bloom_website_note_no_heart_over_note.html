A few helpful things

Ordering information

A few simple notes so every order goes smoothly.

♡ How to order

Send us a direct message on Instagram with the treats and flavours you would like, quantities, and your preferred collection or delivery option.

♡ Deposit & payment

A 50% deposit is required to confirm your order. The remaining 50% is due on collection or delivery. Payment by bank transfer, Bizum or cash in person.

♡ Collection & delivery

Collection and local delivery are available in Esplugues de Llobregat and nearby areas, including Can Vidalet. Times are agreed when ordering. Please ask us about availability and any delivery cost outside the area.

♡ Changes & cancellations

Please request changes at least 24 hours in advance; changes depend on availability. Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours before the agreed time. For cancellations with less than 24 hours’ notice, the deposit is non-refundable under the terms confirmed with your order.

♡ Allergens

Our products may contain or come into contact with nuts, gluten, milk, eggs and soy. Please tell us about any allergies before ordering and ask for product-specific allergen information.

♡ Freshness & storage

Every treat is handmade to order. Store in a cool, dry place and refrigerate products when advised. Please follow the storage instructions provided and enjoy within the recommended timeframe.
